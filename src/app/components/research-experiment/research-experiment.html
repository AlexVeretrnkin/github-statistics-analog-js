Methodology & provenance

How the experiment was evaluated

Data preparation
Initial partial source months and leading zero months are excluded; all later zeros and anomalies are retained. No interpolation or cleaning. This series uses {{ series.preparation.months }} months, {{ series.preparation.start }} — {{ series.preparation.end }} ({{ series.preparation.excluded_leading_zeros }} leading zero months removed; initial partial month {{ series.preparation.excluded_initial_partial ? 'excluded' : 'not present' }}).
Expanding-window validation
24 common origins from September 2023 through August 2025, with at least 36 training months. Each origin labels a completed month; the cutoff is month end. All models use the same training and target data. Structural choices are repeated inside each training window. A failed fit would exclude the origin for all models; no fits failed.
Naive and seasonal naive
Naive repeats the last observation: X̂(T+h|T) = X(T). Seasonal naive repeats the prior annual cycle: X̂(T+h|T) = X(T+h−12) for h ≤ 12. Both use the same log1p scale. At h = 12 the point predictions coincide; their uncertainty can differ.
SARIMA
KPSS differencing, seasonal-strength seasonal differencing, period 12, stepwise AICc search with approximation disabled. CSS initializes maximum likelihood (CSS–ML, BFGS). Mean/drift permitted when applicable. Selected seasonal orders can be zero.
ETS
Likelihood estimation of smoothing parameters and initial states with the library's internal simplex optimizer; AICc structure selection with usual and admissible bounds. Eligible damped/undamped trends and seasonality are searched; multiplicative trends are disabled.
Prophet
MAP linear trend, 25 candidate changepoints in the first 80% of training history, additive annual Fourier order 10. Prior scales: changepoints 0.05, seasonality 10. No weekly/daily terms, holidays or external regressors. Newton below 100 observations, otherwise L-BFGS with Newton fallback. No hyperparameter search or MCMC. 1,000 seeded predictive draws supply nested 80% and 95% quantiles; full posterior parameter uncertainty is not included.
Scores and uncertainty
RMSE = √mean(error²); MAE = mean(|error|); WAPE = 100 × Σ|error| / Σactual. Errors are scored at exactly h months, never pooled across series. Interval score = width + 2/α × missed distance below/above the interval, with α = 0.20 or 0.05. Lower scores balance width and misses. Inverse mapping is max(expm1(z), 0), without mean bias adjustment.
